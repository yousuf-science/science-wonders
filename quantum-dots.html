The Core Concept: Confinement

Everything you just played with is driven by a single rule of physics: Quantum Confinement. If you trap a tiny bit of electricity (an electron) in a microscopic space, the size of that space controls its energy and color.

Why does size equal frequency?

In the quantum world, an electron acts like a wave. Imagine pushing the walls of a room closer together while a wave is bouncing between them. The wave gets squished. A squished wave is forced to have a shorter wavelength and a higher frequency. Higher frequency light carries more energy, appearing as Blue or Violet. If you expand the room, the wave stretches out, dropping in frequency to become Red or even invisible Infrared.
